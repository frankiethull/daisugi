## Glossary

daisugi is designed as an engine curation metarepo. there are a handful
of nomenclature below that will be helpful for navigating the package.
all machines are fitted with (x, y) interfaces. the machines are
dictated with a prefix verb. Those are listed below:

### Keywords

##### fit machines with: *grow\_*

##### infer machines with: *harvest\_*
